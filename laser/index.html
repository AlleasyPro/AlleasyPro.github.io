The Challenges of Heavy Tube Cutting
When running high-volume tube cutting lines, fabricators face massive setup bottlenecks:
Massive Dust & Slag Volumetrics: Continuous tube piercing produces high volumes of fine metallic dust and fumes that can clog standard shops and breach local EPA/municipal environmental bylaws.
Heavy Component Loads: Automatic loaders and bundle racks weigh tons, requiring reinforced shop floors and heavy-duty overhead structural support.
High Sparks & Fire Hazard: Intense sparks entering the exhaust ductwork pose a severe fire risk to the filter elements.
