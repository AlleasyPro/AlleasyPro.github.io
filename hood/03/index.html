  1. First Impression
    Tailor-Made Ecology Unit (PCU) Design & Installation Services
    From complex compliance engineering to seamless on-site installation.
    We design and build certified commercial kitchen ventilation systems that guarantee odor-free, low-level discharge compliance.
  2. The Core Value: Why Design Matters (The Problem We Solve)
    Explain why clients cannot just buy an off-the-shelf box without your expertise:
    Space Constraints: Kitchen roofs and basements have limited space; every PCU requires custom ductwork routing and structural sizing.
    Static Pressure Calculation: Improper design leads to weak kitchen suction or burning fan motors. We calculate exact airflow demands.
    Strict Regulations: Local codes dictate exactly where and how cleaned air can be discharged. Our designs guarantee passing inspections.

  1. Our Step-by-Step Execution Workflow
    Use a numbered timeline layout to showcase your full-service capabilities:
    Site Survey: We measure kitchen volume, hood sizes, and analyze structural load capacities.
    Custom CFM Design: We calculate total airflow and engineer the exact multi-stage filtration mix (ESP, Carbon, HEPA).
    3D CAD Layout: You receive detailed 3D drafting blueprints showing exactly how the PCU integrates with your building.
    Professional Installation: Our experienced technician team handles rigging, duct connections, electrical wiring, and fan balancing.
    Testing & Commissioning: We measure air velocity, test pressure drops, and issue compliance certification.

  2. Custom Design Capabilities
    Use a structured table to show how you adapt your designs to different client constraints:

Installation Type Best Used For Design Focus
Rooftop Mount Malls, high-rise buildings Weatherproofing, wind-load framing, structural integrity
Indoor / Ceiling Suspended Basements, tight urban venues Space optimization, noise dampening, vibration isolation
Side-Wall Low Discharge Historic buildings, no roof access Maximum odor/smoke removal to protect adjacent windows

  1. We offer a one-stop scheduled maintenance service for commercial kitchen and industrial PCU (Ecology Units), keeping your system compliant and running at peak efficiency:
    Deep Cleaning & Replacement: Routine wash of metal pre-filters and electrostatic cells (ESP), plus fast replacement of MERV 15, HEPA, and activated carbon filters.
    Safety & Performance Checks: Clearing drain lines/sumps to prevent fire risks, cleaning high-voltage insulators to stop false alarms, and inspecting fan belts and seals.
    Compliance & Fire Safety: Testing fire suppression interlocks and providing complete, audit-ready service logs for local fire and environmental inspections.

To get a tailored service proposal, please let us know your location or the number of PCU units you have.
