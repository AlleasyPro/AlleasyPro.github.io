2-in-1 Convertible Industrial Spray BoothsSwitch Between Open-Face & Enclosed Modes in Seconds.
Engineered for ultimate versatility in Toronto workshops. Get the fast access of an open booth and the dust-free precision of an enclosed cabin—all in a single, space-saving footprint.

Mode 1: Open-Face Efficiency
When to use: Perfect for high-volume primer spraying, sanding, or finishing smaller components, furniture parts, and metal fabrications.The Benefit: Maximum accessibility. Your team can move parts in and out rapidly without opening and closing main doors, boosting daily production speed and reducing ventilation energy costs.

Mode 2: Fully Enclosed Precision
When to use: Ideal for high-gloss topcoats, sensitive materials, or whenever your project demands a flawless, mirror-like finish.The Benefit: Seal the cabin instantly to create a pressurized, 100% controlled environment. High-efficiency intake filters eliminate airborne dust and contaminants before they ever touch your wet paint.

HARDWARE & ONTARIO COMPLIANCE
Stationary, Heavy-Duty Build: Unlike flimsy temporary tents, our booths feature a rigid, stationary frame made of precision-engineered, galvanized tongue-and-groove steel panels for maximum durability.
Smart Airflow Control: The ventilation system automatically adjusts pressure and airflow patterns when switching modes, ensuring consistent overspray capture.
ESA & CSA Certified Components: All fans, explosion-proof motors, and control panels are fully CSA approved / ULc listed to guarantee seamless Electrical Safety Authority (ESA) site sign-off in Ontario.
NFPA 33 Compliant: Engineered strictly to meet local Ontario Fire Codes for commercial and industrial paint application
